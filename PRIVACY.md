PRIVACY POLICY - DUNGEON EXPLORER

Your privacy is our priority. 

- Data Collection: This application collects zero telemetry, system diagnostics, or personal information. 
- Local Storage: All data, including Accounts, Friends, and Notes, is stored exclusively on your local device using encrypted methods (Windows DPAPI). 
- Transmission: No data is transmitted to external servers. Your gaming experience remains completely local and private.

While Dungeon Explorer does not host a central server or collect personal telemetry, it communicates with third-party APIs (e.g., DDO Audit, GitHub, SSG Authentication Servers, Akamai, Microsoft) to provide live data and assets. Your interaction with these services is governed by their respective privacy policies. No credentials or personal data are ever shared with these third parties by the Software.
